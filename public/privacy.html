2. What we collect

  • Account: e-mail address and a password hash (handled by our database provider, Supabase).
  • Your content: dreams, goals, habits, daily progress, rewards, calendar events, images you upload or link.
  • Device data for reminders and the widget: notification settings and a random widget token. We do not use advertising IDs.
  • Purchases: which plan you have and its validity, received from Google Play / our payment provider. We never see your card number.
  • AI features (Pro + AI only): the messages you send to the AI coach and the photos or prompts you use for image generation.

3. Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the app and sync your data across devices — performance of our contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To process payments and keep records required by tax law — legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To keep the service secure and fix errors — legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. AI features

When you use the AI coach, your messages and the relevant parts of your habits and goals are sent to Anthropic (Claude API) to generate replies. When you generate images, your prompt and any photo you choose (for example a selfie) are sent to Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI. These providers process the data on our behalf to return a result. Only upload photos of yourself or of people who agreed. You can report any generated output from within the app.
